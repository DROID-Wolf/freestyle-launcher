TonightSunset was at 6:31. Clear skies after midnight, a good night to project outside.
  • What's new vs. the first mockup: slow-moving aurora background, a live clock with a blinking colon, a sunrise→sunset progress bar, a glass weather card with an animated icon and hourly forecast, a dock you can move along with the arrow keys and labels on focus, and a rotating line of text at the bottom right.
  • Customizable quick apps: the small ✎ button at the top right (press ↑ on the remote) opens a picker. Choose up to 6 apps in the order you want, and press Back or Done to save. The projector remembers your choice. In the real app, the picker lists the apps actually installed on the projector.
  • Time-of-day themes: the colors shift on their own through morning, day, evening and night.
  • The animations only move and scale shapes, which stays smooth on the Freestyle's modest chip. If it stutters on the real projector, I can turn the background blur down.
  • Weather numbers here are sample data. The real app pulls them from a free weather service for your city.
